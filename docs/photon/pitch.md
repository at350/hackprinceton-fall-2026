Lucid is the third person in the hand-off.
A medic scans a head injury, and Lucid finds a likely bleed.
Once the medic approves, the on-call neurosurgeon gets the bleed map and a pre-alert on iMessage.
No app, no login.
The surgeon texts a question; Lucid answers from the scan, and the medic sees both.
The surgeon texts ACCEPT, and the medic sees it.
If the surgeon stays silent for a minute, Lucid calls them and texts a recap.
